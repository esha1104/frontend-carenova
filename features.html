CareNova Features

Enhanced Health Awareness

Digital health applications and ad campaigns allow users to explore reliable resources. Enhanced health awareness includes understanding health challenges, adopting healthy habits, and staying informed.

Streamlined Diagnosis Prediction

Using AI and ML, this system analyzes patient data to accurately predict potential diagnoses. It accelerates diagnosis, reduces human error, and empowers doctors with decision-support tools.

Empowered Health Decisions

Individuals use digital tools and health data to actively guide their care, leading to better treatment understanding and improved health autonomy and outcomes.

Accessible Health Information

Accessibility ensures that everyone can obtain clear and accurate medical information. With online health records and multi-language content, this feature promotes informed decisions.

Included Functional Features

  • Multiple symptom input
  • Diagnosis predictions
  • Information on potential diseases
  • Direct messaging
  • Disease awareness
  • Interactive symptom checker
